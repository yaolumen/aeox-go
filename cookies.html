Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Local storage works similarly but can store larger amounts of data in your browser. This policy covers both technologies.

2. Cookies We Use

AEOX uses very few cookies and local storage. We do not use any third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

2.1 Essential Cookies

CookiePurposeDurationType
connect.sidAdmin panel session authentication. Only set when an admin logs in.4 hours of inactivityFirst-party, Essential

2.2 Local Storage

KeyPurposeDurationType
dl_cache_*Caches download link information so you can recover purchases without re-entering your email24 hoursFirst-party, Functional

Local storage data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third party. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

3. Cookies We Do NOT Use

For transparency, here is what we explicitly do not use:

  • Analytics cookies: No Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar
  • Advertising cookies: No Facebook Pixel, Google Ads, or retargeting
  • Social media cookies: No social sharing or login widgets
  • Third-party cookies: No cookies from external domains (except Stripe during checkout, which operates under its own cookie policy)

4. Stripe Checkout Cookies

When you make a purchase, you are redirected to Stripe's checkout page. Stripe may set their own cookies as part of the payment process. These are governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy, not ours. We have no control over Stripe's cookies.

5. Managing Cookies

5.1 Browser Controls

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

5.2 Clearing Local Storage

To clear AEOX's local storage data:

  • Open your browser's Developer Tools (F12)
  • Go to Application → Local Storage → go.aeox.uk
  • Delete entries starting with dl_cache_

5.3 Effect of Blocking Cookies

Since we use so few cookies, blocking them will have minimal impact:

  • Blocking connect.sid: Admin panel login will not work (does not affect regular visitors)
  • Blocking local storage: Download link recovery via browser cache will not work; you will need to use the email recovery form instead

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever introduce new cookies or tracking technologies, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date. We will never introduce advertising or tracking cookies without explicit notification.

7. Contact

Questions about our cookie usage? Contact us at support@aeox.uk. For corporate or legal inquiries, contact contact@yaolumen.com.
